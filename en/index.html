01 · About

From experiment to application.

As an AI Consultant at atrete, I focus on how artificial intelligence and data automation can create tangible value within organisations. My work with AI began more than five years ago with machine learning and computer vision and now spans generative AI and agent-based applications in enterprise environments.

As an extended board member at SwissAI, an entrepreneurship enthusiast and host of the podcast “Want to be Entrepreneurs!”, I also explore how new technologies are changing companies, ways of working and business models.

My approach is pragmatic: understand new technologies, experiment with them and turn them into applications that work in practice. On stage, I connect technological developments with hands-on experience and real examples.
