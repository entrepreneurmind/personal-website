01 Approach

From experiment to application

My work with AI began more than five years ago with machine learning and computer vision. Today it is about generative AI and agents in enterprise environments, and about how new technologies change companies, ways of working and business models.

01 · Understand

Explain it first

I dig into a topic until I can explain it without slides. That is my bar before I recommend anything.

02 · Try

Built myself before I advise

What I recommend, I have tried myself first. My own agent system runs every day and is my lab for it.

03 · Deliver

Until it runs day to day

At atrete, I guide organisations from strategy and use-case development to practical implementation.
